03 · Hold Anatomy

Six shapes. Six hand positions.

Holds carry meta-information through their shape, not just their color. Players read them at a glance: a jug means rest, a crimp means commit, a chip means a foothold-only.

Jug

Restable

Big and incut. Use as breath markers — places players regroup between actions.

Sloper

Friction

Wide and rounded. No edge. Costs more stamina — but no risk of slipping if you commit.

Pinch

Grip cost

Held with thumb opposed. Costs chalk every turn it's used. Great for stability.

Crimp

Risk hold

Small edge. High commitment. A failed crimp roll costs double stamina.

Pocket

Specialty

One- or two-finger hole. Triggers special abilities for characters with finger-strength traits.

Foot chip

Foot only

Small. Foot placements only — never countable as a hand hold. Free to use, can't be removed.

04 · Volumes

Geometric features. Wall changes.

Volumes are bigger than holds — geometric prisms bolted to the wall that reshape the surface itself. In game terms: terrain modifiers. They occupy multiple grid cells and change which holds can be placed near them.

07 · Motion

Every hold has weight.

Animations should feel like you can feel them through the screen. Tactile, weighted, a little resistant. Nothing floats. Nothing pops. Things press, settle, dust.

— grab + press

Hold Press

Tap a hold → it shifts down 2px and the inset shadow deepens for 80ms, then settles. Like pressing flesh into rubber. No bounce.

— particles

Chalk Burst

On a successful send, the touched hold puffs 8–12 small chalk particles upward, drifting and fading over 1.4s. Cheap fluid simulation, not a confetti pop.

— route reveal

Tape Trace

Route lines reveal stroke-by-stroke from base to top, ~900ms ease-out, with a subtle dash crawl. Like watching a setter mark a problem.

— hover

Bolt Wiggle

Hover any hold → it rotates −3° and lifts 3px in 250ms. Settles back on leave. Subtle but full of life — holds aren't decorative.

— failure

Whip Fall

A failed crux roll: the active card shakes laterally for 200ms then drops 60px with gravity easing. Recovers with a faint dust-rise on the wall behind it.

— ambient

Wall Breathing

The wall texture has a 6-second ambient ripple — barely visible, just enough to feel the room is alive. The grit shifts 1px on a slow sine.
