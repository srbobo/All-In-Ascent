    How it works

    Rules are built from elements.

    1. Start a card and set its numbers: a climber's stats and growth, a route's requirements and dice, a gear card's price.
    2. Give climbers and gear rules: pick conditions (on Overhang routes, when the route needs under 30 Strength, at Level 3 or more…) and effects (+5 Strength, the climb costs −1 Time, roll an extra buff die…). Add, remove and reorder them freely.
    3. The card compiles against the rules engine as you type. A card that compiles plays in your local games exactly as its text says.
    4. Happy with it? Submit it for review and the designer sees it in the review queue.

    Rules engine v—

    Submitted cards are sent with the name and note you give and nothing else.
